title: Launch pad boards through design review
---

I am leading the redesign of the USYD Rocketry Team's launch pad electronics for Kookaburra, the team's next rocket, as a modular set of custom boards on one RS-485 bus.

The two boards I designed, a 12-channel valve control board and the umbilical board that links the pad to the rocket, are through the team's Critical Design Review with schematic and 4-layer layout complete. Next comes fabrication and bring-up. [More on the project page.](/projects/gse-pad-box/)
